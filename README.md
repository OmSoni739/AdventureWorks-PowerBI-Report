AdventureWorks Sales & Customer Intelligence Dashboard
Built with Microsoft Power BI | Dataset by Maven Analytics

📌 About the Dataset
AdventureWorks Cycles is a fictional global manufacturing company created by Maven Analytics for business intelligence training. The dataset simulates real-world operations of a bicycle and accessories company operating across North America, Europe, and the Pacific region.
It covers 2020–2022 and includes:

Sales transactions — orders, revenue, returns, and profit across product lines
Customer data — 17,400+ unique customers segmented by income level and occupation
Product catalog — Bikes, Accessories, and Clothing with individual SKU-level performance
Territory data — Sales broken down by country and regional territory


📊 What I Built
A 6-page interactive Power BI report that transforms raw transactional data into executive-ready insights. Each page serves a distinct analytical purpose.
